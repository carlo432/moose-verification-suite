# T2-16 — Darcy flow / Theis drawdown

This case starts from MOOSE's gold-backed PorousFlow `theis1.i` single-sink input. It runs three mesh resolutions and retains the emitted pressure/mass CSV data. It also runs the shipped axisymmetric `theis_rz.i` formulation on a 200-cell uniform radial mesh, removing the square-domain corners for a direct Theis fit.

The intended reference is Theis (1935), `s=(Q/(4πT))E₁(r²S/(4Tt))`. Dividing the seed mass equation by the reference density gives an independently derived mapping `S=φ/K=1e-10 1/Pa` and `T=k/μ=1e-11 m²/(Pa·s)` for the square seed, while the radial seed has `S=2.5e-11 1/m`. The axisymmetric fit uses 11 sampled points over `r=10–60 m` and recovers `T=1.06e-11` (6.5% high) and `S=2.64e-11` (5.6% high), still outside the predeclared 2% acceptance. Their identifiable ratio `T/S=0.4034 m²/s` is within 0.85% of the mapped `0.4 m²/s`; separate T and S remain ill-conditioned. The square-domain fit and Cooper–Jacob estimate remain diagnostic, and the outer-boundary check finds 12.6 Pa far-field drawdown. An independent mass ledger confirms the imposed 40 kg withdrawal to 2.8e-6 relative error on every mesh; the result remains PARTIAL because the transient acceptance comparison is not closed.
